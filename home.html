FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Are League of Legends custom skins safe to use? ▼
Yes! Custom skins are safe to use and follow Riot Games' policies. They only change what you see on your screen and don't affect gameplay or other players' experience.
Will other players see my custom skin in game? ▼
No, custom skins are client-side only. Only you can see your custom skins in-game. Other players will see the default or official skins.
How do I install League custom skins? ▼
Visit our Mods page, choose your favorite skin, and follow the installation instructions. Most skins come with detailed guides to help you get started.
What if a League patch breaks my custom skin? ▼
After major patches, some custom skins may need updates. Check back on our website for updated versions, or join our Discord for announcements.
